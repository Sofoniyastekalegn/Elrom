
Major Pulses (Legumes)

• Faba bean (broad bean): The largest pulse crop by production volume, grown mostly in cooler highlands.
• Haricot / Common bean: Widely grown in warmer mid- and low-altitude areas, including white pea beans, red/light speckled kidney beans, pinto beans, and cream or black beans.
• Chickpea (Shimbra): Grown in both Desi (smaller, darker) and Kabuli (larger, lighter) varieties, used for shiro, kik, and kolo.
• Field pea: A major cool-season highland pulse used for food and animal feed.
• Lentil: Grown in cool highlands and widely used in kik wot, soups, and azifa.
• Grass pea (Guaya): A hardy pulse grown in highland and food-security areas.
• Other pulses: Mung bean, soybean, cowpea, pigeon pea, and lupin.

Major Seeds (Oilseeds & Spice Seeds)

• Sesame: A key export oilseed, including Humera and Wollega types.
• Noug (Niger seed): A traditional Ethiopian oilseed crop.
• Linseed (flaxseed): Commonly cultivated as an oilseed in highland areas.
• Groundnut (peanut) & Safflower: Important oilseed crops in suitable warmer or drier zones.
• Ethiopian mustard (Gomenzer): Grown both for leafy greens and oilseed.
• Fenugreek & Black cumin (Nigella): Widely used spice and medicinal seeds.
