Commodities on this floor

  • Coffee: 100% Coffea arabica, sold by region and process
  • Pulses: faba bean, chickpea, lentil, field pea, and grass pea
  • Beans: haricot and common beans, including white pea, kidney, pinto, and black beans
  • Seeds: sesame, noug, linseed, groundnut, safflower, fenugreek, and black cumin

Region notes and cup profiles are in the blog. Grading rules are on the about page.

How a bid is placed

  1. Open the auction list and choose a lot number.
  2. Enter your buyer name, email, and bid in USD per metric ton.
  3. Attach your trade licence or company profile with the file field.
  4. Submit the form. Your email program opens so you can send the bid to the trading desk.
